## About the Collection

This is a personal collection of fifteen finished cross stitch designs, each carefully handmade and stitched by me, Victoria Pavik. Each piece reflects time, patience, learning, and attention to detail, coming together as a showcase of creativity. The collection highlights both the artistry of traditional cross stitching and the individuality behind each design. What makes this collection the most meaningful to me is the visual progress I have made in my stitching skills over the years, and the memories that come with each finished piece.
